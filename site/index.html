Deterministic verification for distributed systems

Make rare distributed-system failures reproducible.

Promtact turns race conditions, network faults and persistence failures into deterministic tests your team can replay locally, in CI and inside its own infrastructure.

Self-hosted. Evidence-backed. No required control plane.

Outcomes

What your team gets

Move correctness work from incident reconstruction into a repeatable engineering workflow with a result that can be shared, reviewed and rerun.

01

Reproduce the exact failure

Turn a rare production-like interleaving into a stable seed, step and trace your engineers can run again.

The reproduction can travel with the issue, giving the engineer who owns the fix the same execution that exposed it.

02

Make correctness a CI gate

Run declared faults and safety properties on every relevant change instead of relying on occasional manual fault testing.

A failing property can block the relevant change with a stable reproduction instead of a flaky symptom.

03

Review evidence, not anecdotes

Keep versioned scenarios, traces and qualification results with clearly stated technical bounds.

Reviewers can inspect the inputs, the result and the stated limits without reconstructing a verbal incident history.

Workflow

From protocol to reproducible evidence

Promtact focuses the campaign on the behavior that matters, then preserves any violation as an execution your team can revisit.

  1. 1

    Connect your system

    Model or adapt the protocol behavior that matters to your correctness boundary.

  2. 2

    Define properties and faults

    Describe what must never happen and which partitions, delays, losses or failures should be exercised.

  3. 3

    Replay every violation

    Return to the same seed, step and trace locally, in CI or during review.

Start with the open-source framework or use the Enterprise path when installation, operations and qualification must fit a controlled self-hosted environment.

Enterprise

Operate a controlled self-hosted product

Use a verified distribution and lifecycle tooling when the verification environment must stay inside your infrastructure and meet an explicit operating boundary.

The Enterprise path adds the operational controls around the same deterministic model; it does not move executions or evidence into a required hosted service.

Trust

Built to be checked

Promtact separates product claims from their evidence. Releases, scenarios and qualification results state which behavior was tested, on which commit, and within which bounds.

  • Deterministic replay
  • Bounded verification
  • Signed release artifacts
  • Self-hosted execution

The objective is not a blanket promise that distributed software cannot fail. It is a narrower and checkable contract: define the safety boundary, exercise declared faults, retain the exact execution and state what the resulting evidence does—and does not—establish.

Because execution remains inside the team’s environment, its scenarios and results stay available under the organization’s own access, retention and review controls.
