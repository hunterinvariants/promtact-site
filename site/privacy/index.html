Privacy

Last reviewed 17 August 2026

Promtact is software you download and run yourself. This page covers the only two places personal data can arise: this website, and writing to us.

The software sends nothing back. There is no telemetry, no phone home, no licence check that transmits usage, and no endpoint here for it to reach. Your seeds, traces, scenarios and measurements stay on your machine. You can confirm that from the source rather than from this sentence.

Controller

Promtact, Zurich, Switzerland. contact@promtact.com.

This website

promtact.com sets no cookies, loads no fonts, scripts, images or anything else from a third party, and runs no analytics. Its content security policy is default-src 'none', which forbids every outbound request and is checkable in the response headers rather than promised here.

The hosting provider records ordinary server logs - address, user agent, referrer, time, requested path. These are technically necessary to serve the site and to keep it available. They are not combined with anything else and are not used to build a profile.

Your rights

Under the revised Swiss Data Protection Act and, where it applies, the GDPR, you may ask what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, ask for a copy, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to contact@promtact.com.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner, and in the EU the authority for your country of residence.
